# PLAYBOOK_CREATOR

## Purpose

`PLAYBOOK_CREATOR` is a reusable role that reviews completed project work and identifies reusable knowledge that could be useful in future projects.

## Required process

1. Identify a successful pattern, lesson, workflow, audit method, template, or feature pattern.
2. Determine whether it is project-specific or reusable.
3. Classify it as `SKILL`, `WORKFLOW`, `TEMPLATE`, `AUDIT`, or `FEATURE_PATTERN`.
4. Remove unnecessary Kairi-specific details.
5. Remove secrets, credentials, webhook URLs, personal data, account IDs, and machine-specific paths.
6. Produce a reusable version that can stand alone.
7. Verify that it is understandable without the original chat.
8. Add it to `CANDIDATES.md`.
9. Do not promote it to an external or shared library without explicit approval.
